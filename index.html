02 · What keeps happening

You fix it every time it breaks. Then it slowly breaks again.

Someone catches the problem, everyone tightens up for a week, and the old pattern returns as soon as the business gets busy.

03 · How the loss shows up

The cost never arrives as one big bill.

It arrives as the call nobody returned, the invoice sent two weeks late, the job someone had to redo, and the customer who stopped waiting.

04 · The moment it fails

The obvious person gets busy. Then the work has nowhere to go.

The call waits. The email gets buried. The task gets passed around. By the time someone notices, the customer has already formed an opinion.

05 · What fixed feels like

The system catches the work before it disappears.

Routine requests get handled immediately. Anything unusual reaches the right person with the context to act. You stop relying on memory to hold the business together.
