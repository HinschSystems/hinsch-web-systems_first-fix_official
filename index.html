What we believe

Complexity should not get in the way of what people are trying to do. Technology should help people and organizations do more, with greater clarity and control.

Our approach

A shared, current picture of what is happening

Leads, jobs, payments, and deadlines stay current as work happens. Everyone can work from the same up-to-date record. We call that a Live Business State. Each workflow we build reads from it and records its updates there.

What it is

A current, organized record of what is happening in your business.

It updates as work happens, so the record does not depend on someone remembering to update it later.

What it is not
  • · A report you run on Friday
  • · A spreadsheet someone keeps by hand
  • · Something that lives in one person's head
  • · The AI's memory

How the record gets updated

Every change is checked before it is saved.

1

A person or an AI tool requests a change.

2

The system checks whether the person or tool is allowed to make the change, and whether it fits the current situation.

3

The change is saved along with who made it and when.

4

Everyone can see the updated record.

AI can suggest a change. Your system checks it against your rules before saving it.

Why it matters

AI projects often struggle because of the work around the AI.

Gartner expects most agentic AI projects to be cancelled in the next couple of years. Often the problem is how the AI is connected to the work: there is no shared, current record, no one checking its work before it acts, and small mistakes can build up when it keeps going without review.

This is a math example, not a measurement of a real system. If each step is right 95% of the time, chaining 15 steps with no checks in between means the odds of every step going right fall to about 46%, worse than a coin flip. Checking each step before the next begins can prevent one unchecked mistake from carrying through the rest of the process. That is why checkpoints matter.

Small errors can add up

Ten or fifteen steps run in a row with no one checking the work. Even steps that usually work can go wrong.

Working from outdated information

Without one current picture of the business that people can trust, AI may act on outdated or incomplete information.

Work goes out without review

A small mistake can become a real problem if no one reviews the work first. A person should be able to check it before it goes out.

Gartner, IDC, and MIT research point to recurring challenges: how AI is connected to the work, whether the data is ready, and who is responsible for reviewing it. These are the parts we help build around the AI.
