How the record gets updated

Every change is checked before it is saved.

  1. A person or an AI tool requests a change.
  2. The system checks whether the person or tool is allowed to make the change, and whether it fits the current situation.
  3. The change is saved along with who made it and when.
  4. Everyone can see the updated record.

AI can suggest a change. Your system checks it against your rules before saving it.

Closed-Loop Workflow
